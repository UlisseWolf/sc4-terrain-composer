Includes: automatic detection of connected land blobs (via gdal:polygonize), click or lasso selection, move with edge feathering, delete with diffusion-based inpainting, rotate (selection or export-only), undo/redo, an export-rectangle preview, an optimal-rotation finder, and final export snapped to the 64x+1 / 256x+1 dimensions SC4 requires (or the power-of-2 sizes OpenTTD requires), with automatic elevation scale-factor adjustment when the terrain exceeds the chosen ceiling.
